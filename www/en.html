Guide.

You say when you want to eat. Tempasto tells you when to do what.

1
Plan
  • On Start your plan is a single sentence. Tap a word underlined in red to change it.
  • Below it you see the key steps. Tap them for the full schedule.
2
Pizzas and shopping
  • Under Pizzas you choose what gets baked with +. Your own pizzas are at the end of the menu.
  • The shopping list writes itself. Long-press a line to mark it “always have it”.
3
Baking
  • Start plan switches on the reminders. Bake mode then guides you step by step.
  • Behind Done there’s a note from Nonna. She explains every step in detail.
  • If things change, Running late? moves the whole plan.
4
Settings
  • Set oven, dough ball, yeast and kneading under More → Recipe & oven. Preferment, flour, temperatures and units are in expert mode.

Story.

Why Tempasto?

Naples, Spaccanapoli, just before eight. The alley is so narrow the washing lines almost touch. A scooter rattles past, a nonna calls down from a balcony. And then you smell it: wood smoke, charred crust, sweet tomatoes. There’s a queue outside a door with no sign.

Inside, a man works without looking up. He presses the dough flat with his fingers, stretches it over the backs of his hands and slides it into the embers. Ninety seconds later the best pizza of your life is in front of you. You ask him for the secret. He shrugs: “Il tempo e l’impasto.” Time and dough. From the balcony you hear the nonna again. He grins: you should ask less and eat more.

His secret is in the name: Tempasto, from tempo and impasto. Nobody can give you the time, but you don’t have to keep it in your head. Tempasto works backwards and lets you know when the dough needs you.

And because your first dough still raises questions, Nonna is there. She knows when the water is too warm, when you’ve kneaded enough and when to leave the dough alone.

The rest is up to you, with your hands. Fatto a mano means “made by hand”; in Naples a rolling pin never touches the dough. Tempasto plans, Nonna helps, you make the pizza.

Help.

Wrong yeast amount? Dough checkWhen will my dough be ripe, and what now?
–

Rescue the doughToo much yeast or over-proofed
  • Chill it right away and divide it; single balls cool down faster.
  • Fridge to the coldest setting, boxes at the back and bottom, not stacked.
  • Freeze as an emergency brake: balls one by one in oiled bags. On baking day into the fridge 6–8 h ahead, then 2–3 h at room temperature.
  • Warm up for only 1–2 h instead of 3–5 h.
  • Over-proofed dough: stretch carefully on a floured counter, not over your fists.
What went wrong?Dough tears, flat rim, pale base …
  • Dough tears when stretchingUsually it’s still too cold. Leave the balls at room temperature for 20–30 minutes longer. Don’t pull, press from the centre outwards.
  • Dough keeps shrinking backThe gluten is still tense. Let it rest covered for 15–20 minutes, then it works.
  • Balls spread out and stickThey’re over-proofed or too warm. Keep them cooler, use more semola when stretching and bake quickly. See also “Rescue the dough”.
  • Rim stays flatThe air was pressed out of the rim while shaping. Leave a finger’s width of rim untouched and push the air from the middle outwards. A stone that’s too cool also holds the rim back.
  • Base stays pale or softThe stone isn’t hot enough or the toppings are too wet. Preheat longer, drain the fior di latte, use less sauce.
  • Base burns, toppings still rawThe stone is hotter than the air above it. Turn the flame up, let the stone cool briefly, or lift the pizza at the end.
  • Pizza sticks to the peelToo little semola or topped for too long. Dust the peel, top quickly and give it a little shake before launching.
  • Dough warmer than 25 °C after kneadingUse colder water next time. For now, put the dough in the fridge straight after folding.
  • Dough barely risesThe yeast was old or the fridge is very cold. Leave the balls at room temperature longer and wait for the poke test.
Using the appMoving, cancelling, number, reminders

Running late or done early? Tap “Running late?” in bake mode. The whole plan and all reminders move with it.

Cancel the dough with “End” at the top. You then have 10 seconds to undo it.

Change the number: before starting, tap the number of pizzas on the home screen. Once the dough is running, the dough balls are fixed. You can still swap pizzas.

Reminders only come once you start the plan. Then the app lets you know at every step, even when it’s closed.

Underlined words like autolyse or bulk ferment can be tapped for a short explanation.

Tomato sauce
    • Pass the tomatoes through a food mill or crush them by hand. No stick blender, or the sauce turns bitter and pale red.
    • Stir in salt and torn basil, oil at the end.
    • Too watery? Pour off some juice first or reduce it briefly.

    The tin should say “100 % Italian tomatoes”. “Made in Italy” only tells you where it was canned. Good DOP brands include Solania and Strianese.
